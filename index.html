Ph.D. Student in Machine Learning, Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI)

I am advised by Salem Lahlou. I am interested in effective computational paradigms for problems that are intractable yet matter: whether that means finding smarter algorithms, exploiting physical laws, or something we have not yet imagined. My current research focuses on GFlowNets. I received my M.Sc. in Applied Physics (cum laude) from TU Delft and my B.Eng. in Nuclear Engineering from Fudan University.

Vision

I have always believed that, for me, the most meaningful work is to turn science fiction into reality. I am drawn to it not for displays of technical virtuosity, but because what once seemed impossible can often do the most to improve people's lives, and offer people some shelter from life's absurdity.

This, of course, depends on the efforts of social scientists and of each of us, to ensure that these technologies serve human flourishing rather than work against it.

At the same time, I remain deeply curious about Wigner's question: why does mathematics describe the universe with such unreasonable effectiveness? More specifically, to what mathematical structure is our universe isomorphic?
